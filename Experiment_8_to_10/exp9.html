CSS: background-repeat and Links

1. background-repeat property

background-repeat: repeat;
background-repeat: repeat-x;
background-repeat: repeat-y;
background-repeat: no-repeat;

2. Styles for links

  • a:link - an unvisited link
  • a:visited - a link that was already visited
  • a:hover - move the mouse over this link
  • a:active - press and hold this link
